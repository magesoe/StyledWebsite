01 / Our story

A long horizon
needs shared energy.

In the heart of Klintholm Havn, where the wind runs free, the horizon feels endless and the sea is always near, Baltic Blue was born.

Not just an initiative, but a movement: a forward-looking dream rooted in a deep love for the ocean and a growing awareness that something must change.
