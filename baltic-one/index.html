01 / Our story

Born where the wind runs free.

In the heart of Klintholm Havn, where the horizon feels endless and the sea is always near, Baltic Blue was born.

Not just an initiative, but a movement. A forward-looking dream, rooted in a deep love for the ocean — and a growing awareness that something must change.
